\section{Simulation Parameters and Provenance}
\label{app:simparams}

Table~\ref{tab:sim-params} lists the parameters of the simulation with their class; modelling choices that are not single values (latency structure, attempt caps) are listed at the end of the table. The assumed premature-claim rate (0.65) and finish-after-rejection rate (0.35) are swept in Study~H to show how far the results depend on them, and the rates that decide the gate's behaviour are derived from the held-out gate states, not from the design states on which the gate was tuned. The per-scenario data (\texttt{scenarios.csv}, \texttt{recovery\_scenarios.csv}), the results, the evidence ledger with the source and class of every simulated number, and the v1 validation report are in \texttt{benchmarks/simulation/out/heldout\_v2/} (v1: \texttt{out/heldout\_v1/}, identical to \texttt{out/heldout/}, which the v1 decision analysis reads) and \texttt{artifacts/results/}. All are regenerated by \texttt{python -m benchmarks.simulation.simulate\_v2} and \texttt{make\_tables}; the v1 recomputation script (\texttt{independent\_validator.py}, an AI-written arithmetic check, not an independent replication) contains absolute paths of the machine it ran on.
