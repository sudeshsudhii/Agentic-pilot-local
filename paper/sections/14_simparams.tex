\section{Simulation Parameters and Provenance}
\label{app:simparams}

Table~\ref{tab:sim-params} lists every parameter of the simulation with its class. Assumed values were chosen to be neither favourable nor unfavourable to verification: the assumed premature-claim rate (0.65) and finish-after-rejection rate (0.35) are swept in Study~H, and the rates that decide the gate's behaviour are derived from the held-out gate states, not from the design states on which the gate was tuned. The per-scenario data (\texttt{scenarios.csv}, \texttt{recovery\_scenarios.csv}), the results, the evidence ledger with the source and class of every simulated number, and the independent validation report are in \texttt{benchmarks/simulation/out/heldout\_v2/} (v1: \texttt{out/heldout\_v1/}) and \texttt{artifacts/results/}. All are regenerated by \texttt{python -m benchmarks.simulation.simulate\_v2} and \texttt{make\_tables}; the independent validator of v1 contains absolute paths of the machine it ran on.
