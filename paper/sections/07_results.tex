\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

All results in this section concern the browser backend and its components. None measures end-to-end task success, and none involves the computer backend.

\subsection{Browser Completion Gate}

Table~\ref{tab:gate} reports the gate study. On the original code the pre-check and $\Phi$, called directly, withheld completion in 15 of the 23 unsatisfied design states that a dispatch-only rule accepts, and rejected 3 of the 15 satisfied ones. Inside the live \texttt{verify} node the gate was weaker, withholding 14 (empty planner reasoning) or 13 (verbose reasoning): the node let a premature \texttt{complete} mark the pending step done (P1) and took the planner's reasoning for an answer (X2). The other misses had specific causes: a results page for another query (S7), a soft ``page not found'' and a sign-in redirect (N6, N7), an interstitial with no known phrase (C4), a superset of the requested text (T4), a long answer without the requested fact (X4), a click on the wrong element (P3), and a request that triggers no conjunct (N8). Two satisfied pages were blocked as bot checks: an article titled ``Robot'' and a sign-in page with a hidden invisible-reCAPTCHA frame (C5, C6).

After the fixes of Section~\ref{sec:impl}, all three modes agree: the gate withholds 20 of the 23 unsatisfied design states and rejects one satisfied state, a correct 13-character answer that does not repeat the goal's terms (X6). Its three misses are the cases no page-level rule can see: the unknown interstitial, the wrong-element click, and the request without a trigger. Because the fixes were designed on these states, this improvement is optimistic by construction. On the 12 held-out states the fixed gate withholds 2 of 5 unsatisfied states (1 before the fixes) and rejects 1 of 7 satisfied ones (an article about CAPTCHAs, whose title contains the word). It misses an unquoted query for the wrong person, an ``Error 404'' title, and an answer that names the wrong city but repeats the goal's term ``population''. Hand-written rules thus fix the cases they were written for and generalize only partially, which is the main argument for the typed postconditions of Table~\ref{tab:post} and for model-based second opinions (Section~\ref{sec:future}).

The gate is cheap next to inference: the 35 design decisions that evaluate $\Phi$ had a median of 30.6\,ms (maximum 39.5\,ms; each the median of 20 repetitions), and the three that stop at the pre-check took at most 6.3\,ms, mostly spent reading page text through the browser protocol. The fixture pages are small, so real pages will take longer.

\subsection{Routing Behavior}

We evaluated the router at each call site without inference (\texttt{routing\_resolution*.csv}). With the default installation (\texttt{qwen2.5:1.5b}, \texttt{moondream}), every text call resolves to \texttt{qwen2.5:1.5b}, decomposition and multi-step planning through the fallback chain. Before the fixes, keywords matched as substrings, so requests such as ``find a guitar guide'' (``gui'') were routed to \texttt{moondream}; recovery switched the active model to \texttt{qwen2.5:7b} without calling it, and planning then stuck to that model; and a new task inherited the previous task's model. All three are fixed. One trade-off remains: with all configured models installed, isolated calls get their role's first candidate, but within a task stickiness keeps \texttt{deepseek-r1:1.5b}, chosen for intent parsing and profiled as able to plan, for decomposition and planning, so in a normal task, which begins with intent parsing, the planner model is not used. The vision fallback selects its model from its own list.

\subsection{Test Suite}

Without Ollama or web access and with a 240\,s per-test timeout, the repository's suite now collects 129 tests in 25 files, including 19 regression tests for the fixes: 126 pass and 3 fail, all three because they need live websites (one also needs Ollama); the 20 Observatory tests pass, and backend line coverage is 65.0\,\% (before the fixes: 108 tests, 104 passing, 4 failing, one module that failed to import, and 62.0\,\%). Passing tests use no language model: node tests stub the executor and gateway, and the test of the central property, that a failed action is never reported complete, now runs headless instead of hanging. The suite supports claims about component behavior, not task success.
