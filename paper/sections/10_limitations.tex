\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Internal validity} The same AI agent wrote all gate states and labels and designed the fixes on the design states, so the design results are optimistic. Held-out~2 was frozen before testing, but its author knew the gate's rules; independent states and labels would give a less biased estimate. The live mode stands in for the planner with empty or generic reasoning, not model output.

\paragraph{External validity} All gate measurements use small, static pages served offline; real pages are larger and change, and latency will grow. The locality test used a stand-in model server and self-hosted pages and instrumented only the Python processes, so it does not show what a real model server, Chromium or the desktop client send off the machine. The end-to-end harness uses fictional, self-hosted sites, which avoids memorized answers and site drift but is simpler than real sites.

\paragraph{Statistical power} The sets are small: with 22 unsatisfied held-out~2 states the 95\% interval of the withheld share spans 0.23--0.61, and the ablation cannot separate conjuncts that change one or two decisions.

\paragraph{Simulation validity} The simulated results follow from assumptions about planner behaviour, action failures and memory that no measurement supports; paired $p$-values describe the synthetic sample, not the agent. The measured inputs come from small constructed sets (54 held-out gate states, of which 27 unsatisfied and 27 satisfied; 10 memory queries; 6 failure messages), and the proposed components are simulated by assumed rates. The reported model (v2) was revised twice after its first results were known (after a methodology audit, and again after a final review of the recovery paths), so it is not pre-registered; the corrections bring it closer to the code and moved the results in favour of $\Phi$, but the choice of what to correct was made with the results in view. Its sample size (100 scenarios) is chosen, so synthetic significance is partly a matter of that choice. It can show which assumptions matter (Study~H), not which outcome will occur.

\paragraph{Construct validity} FCR and FRR depend on the oracle. The browser predicate is keyed to request wording, so its coverage depends on how users phrase requests; when no keyword fires, it reduces to an error check.

\paragraph{System limitations} Planning with 1.5B--7B models is weak; vision-grounded clicks are not individually checked; three of the four recovery strategies (retry, alternative selector, replan) behave identically, as ordinary re-planning; evidence files carry no integrity protection.
