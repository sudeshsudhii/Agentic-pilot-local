\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Internal validity} One author wrote all gate states and labels and designed the fixes on the design states, so the design results are optimistic. Held-out~2 was frozen before testing, but its author knew the gate's rules; independent states and labels would give a less biased estimate. The live mode stands in for the planner with empty or generic reasoning, not model output.

\paragraph{External validity} All gate measurements use small, static pages served offline; real pages are larger and change, and latency will grow. No result involves a language model or a live website, so the agent is not shown to complete tasks. The end-to-end harness uses fictional, self-hosted sites, which avoids memorized answers and site drift but is simpler than real sites.

\paragraph{Statistical power} The sets are small: with 22 unsatisfied held-out~2 states the 95\% interval of the withheld share spans 0.23--0.61, and the ablation cannot separate conjuncts that change one or two decisions.

\paragraph{Construct validity} FCR and FRR depend on the oracle. The browser predicate is keyed to request wording, so its coverage depends on how users phrase requests; when no keyword fires, it reduces to an error check.

\paragraph{System limitations} Planning with 1.5B--7B models is weak, and deterministic overrides compensate on common patterns. Vision-grounded clicks are not individually checked. Two of the four recovery strategies have no distinct behavior. Evidence files carry no integrity protection.
