\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Agentic Pilot is a local browser agent built on the rule that an executed action is not a completed task: it may report completion only when a predicate over the observed page holds, and it keeps the inputs of that decision as evidence. The paper offers three kinds of evidence, which should be read separately. \emph{Measured at component level:} inside the live loop the predicate withheld most premature completions on the states it was designed on and 41\% on states frozen before testing, at a median of 27\,ms and no model call, so its coverage of new phrasings, not its cost, is the limit; the model client refuses non-local endpoints, and the agent process opened no non-loopback connection in episodes driven by a scripted model stand-in. \emph{Simulated, conditional on stated assumptions:} the gate's benefit depends on how often the planner claims completion prematurely and is paid for in false rejections, and memory and recovery would need a relevance filter and an explicit confirmation check. \emph{Not executed:} an end-to-end evaluation with a language model; the harness for it is released with an oracle validated on scripted trajectories, and its results will decide the questions the simulation can only frame.
