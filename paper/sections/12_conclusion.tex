\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\emph{Measured at component level}, on constructed states in Agentic Pilot's real verification node, the completion predicate withheld 41\% of premature completions on states frozen before testing at 27\,ms and no model call; it was more often correct than a dispatch-only rule but not significantly more than its own error check, so coverage of new phrasings, not cost, is its limit. By default the model client connects only to a loopback endpoint, and with a stand-in model server the agent's Python processes connected only to loopback addresses. \emph{In simulation}, under stated assumptions, the gate's benefit depends on the unmeasured rate of premature claims and is paid for in false rejections. These findings characterize the framework within the evaluated components and simulation conditions.
