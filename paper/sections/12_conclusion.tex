\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\emph{Measured at component level}, on constructed states in Agentic Pilot's real verification node, the completion predicate withheld 41\% of premature completions on states frozen before testing at 27\,ms and no model call; it was more often correct than a dispatch-only rule but not significantly more than its own error check, so coverage of new phrasings, not cost, is its limit. By default the model client connects only to a loopback endpoint, and with a stand-in model server the agent's Python processes connected only to loopback addresses. \emph{In simulation}, under stated assumptions, the gate's benefit depends on the unmeasured rate of premature claims and is paid for in false rejections. \emph{Not executed}: the end-to-end evaluation with a language model, whose harness is released. Future work is that run, per-step postconditions derived from the plan, a local vision-language judge as a second opinion, and independently labelled held-out states.
