\paragraph{H1, false completions} Episodes end in a false completion 19 times with $\Phi$ against 28 without verification (Table~\ref{tab:sim-baseline-short}). In the model the direction is fixed by construction ($\Phi$ only withholds claims that no verification accepts), so the size is the informative quantity: 0.11 fewer false completions per scenario, 2.5--97.5th percentiles 0.05--0.18 over 1{,}000 regenerated sets (Table~\ref{tab:sim-tests}).

\paragraph{H2, decision reliability} On the 74 claims (46 truly complete), $\Phi$ decides correctly in 52 and accepting every claim in 46; $\Phi$ is correct alone on 9 claims and wrong alone on 3 (exact McNemar $p=0.15$). Two of the three false rejections come from scenarios with missing evidence, which $\Phi$ rejects at an \textsc{assumed} rate of 0.8, whereas the measured false-rejection rate is 0 of 24. Across regenerated sets $\Phi$ is better at $p<0.05$ in only 42\%, so H2 is not supported at this size. At the policy level the $\Phi$ configuration ends with 44 tasks achieved and reported against 46.

\paragraph{H3--H5} With a misleading-retrieval rate set to the measured 3 of 10, repeated workflows succeed 19 times of 44 with and without memory; the study is too small to detect the assumed effect. Along the code's recovery paths and with assumed per-attempt success rates, the retries before the recovery engine recover 51 of 93 recoverable failures and the engine 68; the proposed evidence-guided variant reaches 87 only by assumption, and missing confirmations are recovered only by continuation (2 of 20). With an assumed 3\,s per model call, $\Phi$ lengthens the mean episode by 2.6\%, through the re-planning that rejections cause rather than its own 0.1\% of the time, and a model self-check by 14\% (cost only).

\paragraph{Sensitivity} The advantage of verification depends on the unmeasured premature-claim rate $\pi$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:sim-sens}): FCR is 0.14 with $\Phi$ against 0.21 without verification at $\pi=0.3$ and 0.32 against 0.45 at $\pi=0.95$, while FRR stays near 0.14 against 0.08. Halving the derived detection rates moves $\Phi$ about halfway to no verification (Table~\ref{tab:sim-sens}).
