\section{Research Questions and Hypotheses}
\label{sec:hypotheses}

The gaps of Section~\ref{sec:intro} lead to five research questions: does evidence-gated completion reduce false completions (RQ1, G1) and make completion decisions more reliable (RQ2, G1--G2); does memory improve consistency on repeated workflows (RQ3); does evidence-guided recovery improve recovery (RQ4); and what does verification cost (RQ5)? Table~\ref{tab:hyp} states the corresponding hypotheses. At the level of the full agent each is examined here only in simulation (Section~\ref{sec:sim}), which cannot test it but shows what it implies under stated assumptions; H1 and H2 also have \textsc{actual} component-level evidence from the gate study: on held-out~2 the gate withholds 9 of 22 premature completions and is correct on 28 of 42 states against 20 of 42 for a dispatch-only rule ($p=0.021$), but not significantly more often than a detector that applies only the error check $E$ ($p=0.070$; Section~\ref{sec:res-gate}). H5 has \textsc{actual} latency evidence. None is tested at task level in this study; for H4 and enhanced H2 the simulated outcome follows from the assumed behaviour of unimplemented components.
