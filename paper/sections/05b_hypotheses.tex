\section{Research Questions and Hypotheses}
\label{sec:hypotheses}

The gaps of Section~\ref{sec:intro} lead to five research questions: does evidence-gated completion reduce false completions (RQ1, G1) and make completion decisions more reliable (RQ2, G1--G2); does memory improve consistency on repeated workflows (RQ3); does evidence-guided recovery improve recovery (RQ4); and what does verification cost (RQ5)? Table~\ref{tab:hyp} states the corresponding hypotheses. Each is tested in this paper only by simulation (Section~\ref{sec:sim}), which shows what the hypotheses imply under stated assumptions; \emph{none is confirmed empirically}, and the end-to-end protocol of Section~\ref{sec:method-e2e} is the empirical test.
