\section{Research Questions and Hypotheses}
\label{sec:hypotheses}

The gaps of Section~\ref{sec:intro} lead to five research questions: does evidence-gated completion reduce false completions (RQ1, G1) and make completion decisions more reliable (RQ2, G1--G2); does memory improve consistency on repeated workflows (RQ3); does evidence-guided recovery improve recovery (RQ4); and what does verification cost (RQ5)? Table~\ref{tab:hyp} states the corresponding hypotheses. At the level of the full agent each is tested here only by simulation (Section~\ref{sec:sim}), which shows what the hypotheses imply under stated assumptions; H1 and H2 also have \textsc{actual} component-level evidence from the gate study (Section~\ref{sec:res-gate}). \emph{None is confirmed empirically end to end}; the protocol of Section~\ref{sec:method-e2e} is that test. For H4 and the enhanced variant of H2 the simulated outcome follows directly from the assumed behaviour of unimplemented components, so their synthetic $p$-values carry no information beyond those assumptions.
